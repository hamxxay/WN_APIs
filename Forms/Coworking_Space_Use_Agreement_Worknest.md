## 4. Fees, Payment, and Security Deposit
- **4.1 Fee:** The Customer agrees to pay a fee of PKR `[Amount]` on a monthly basis, payable in advance.
- **4.2 Advance Payment:** All payments shall be made in advance and are non-refundable unless otherwise stated.
- **4.3 Late Payment:** Late payments may result in suspension of access without prior notice.
- **4.4 Security Deposit:** The Customer shall deposit an amount equivalent to three (3) months’ rent as a refundable security deposit prior to commencement of access.
- **4.5 Deposit Holding & Adjustment:** The security deposit shall be held by Work Nest (Pvt) Ltd as security for performance of the Customer’s obligations and may be adjusted against unpaid fees, damages, losses, penalties, or breaches of this Agreement.
- **4.6 Refund Terms:** Subject to deductions, the remaining security deposit shall be refunded within `[30]` days of termination and handover of the workspace, without interest.
- **4.7 Forfeiture of Security Deposit:** Work Nest (Pvt) Ltd shall have the right to wholly or partially forfeit the security deposit without prejudice to any other rights or remedies, in the event of:
  - **(a)** Non-payment or delayed payment of monthly fees or other dues;
  - **(b)** Breach of this Agreement, including violation of house rules;
  - **(c)** Failure to provide or maintain valid KYC / AML documentation;
  - **(d)** Involvement in illegal, criminal, money laundering, or prohibited activities;
  - **(e)** Any loss of, damage to, theft of, or impairment of assets, furniture, fixtures, equipment, infrastructure, or property belonging to Work Nest (Pvt) Ltd, whether caused directly or indirectly by the Customer, its employees, agents, invitees, or visitors;
  - **(f)** Failure to observe the required notice period or abrupt vacation of the premises.
- **4.8 Excess Damages:** Forfeiture of the security deposit shall not limit Work Nest (Pvt) Ltd’s right to recover any additional losses, damages, or legal costs exceeding the deposited amount.
